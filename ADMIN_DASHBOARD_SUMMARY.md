## 🚀 **How to Use**

### **Reviewing New Candidates:**
1. Go to **Candidates** tab
2. Filter by "Pending Approval"
3. Click **View** on a candidate
4. Review their:
   - Resume and files
   - Quiz results
   - Experience and preferences
5. Click **Approve** or **Reject**

### **Exporting Data:**
1. Go to **Leads** or **Candidates** tab
2. Apply any filters you want
3. Click **Export CSV** button
4. File downloads automatically

### **Viewing Analytics:**
1. Go to **Overview** tab
2. See all metrics at a glance
3. Scroll down for detailed breakdowns
4. Monitor weekly trends

### **Searching & Filtering:**
1. Use the search bar at the top
2. Select filters from dropdowns
3. Results update instantly
4. Clear filters to see all records
